Steps to protect Planet Earth

  1. Use renewable energy resources like solar energy and wind energy
  2. Reuse, recycle and reduce all renewable resources
  3. Never emit carbon dioxide as it is a greenhouse gas
  4. Plant more and more trees and stop deforestation

Some natural wonders of the Earth

  • The Sahara desert: The longest and hottest desert on the earth
  • The Amazon river:The largest river by volume
  • The Tibetan plateau: The largest and highest plateau on the Earth
